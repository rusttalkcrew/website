Shipped

RustTalk content is end-to-end encrypted: the server only stores and forwards ciphertext. Optional phone calls use unencrypted SIP/RTP; the provider and network path can hear them.

v0.10.0 27 Nov 2026

  • Block someone from their profile, menu or Friends list: their server messages fold away, their voice is muted, and their DMs, pokes and friend requests don't reach you; synced across your devices and hidden from servers.
  • DMs from non-friends wait quietly under Message requests until you accept, delete or block them, with Privacy settings for who can message you.
  • Moderation tools: time out a member for 60 seconds to a week, lock a server down manually or automatically during a raid, and remove a banned person's recent messages.
  • Channel slow mode (5 seconds to 6 hours), per-member rate limits with Relaxed, Standard and Strict presets, and new member rules for a newcomer's first period.
  • Names can no longer contain links or invites; names with links show as "Member" with a short code.
  • On phones, a small self view of your camera stays on screen when your own tile is out of sight; drag it to a corner or minimize it.
  • Choose Automatic, 12-hour or 24-hour time in Settings → Appearance, and on computers opening a channel or DM focuses the message box right away.

v0.9.1 26 Nov 2026

  • Android: HEIC photos work everywhere you pick a picture; they are turned into JPEG on your phone.
  • On phones the sticker panel and keyboard no longer show together, tapping the chat closes the panel, and a long-pressed message stays highlighted while its menu is open.

v0.9.0 26 Nov 2026

  • Send up to 10 files in one message, shown together as one album.
  • The sticker and emoji picker has tabs over a grid of large stickers that always animate; on phones it opens in place of the keyboard and previews a sticker before sending.
  • Click the line above a reply to jump to the message it answers; the line itself is calmer.
  • On phones, press and hold a message for its menu instead of a desktop toolbar.
  • Copy or save a picture from the viewer, large phone photos show in albums and the gallery, and online members are listed first.

v0.8.1 26 Nov 2026

  • Several pictures or videos in one message show together as one album, and the viewer steps through them with the arrow keys, on-screen buttons or a swipe.
  • Large phone photos (up to 48 MP and wide panoramas) show as pictures, the right way up, instead of as a file; files waiting to be sent show as equal tiles.

v0.8.0 26 Nov 2026

  • Phones can upload: attach files and photos, set your profile picture or server icon, and add emoji and stickers with the phone's own picker on Android and iPhone.
  • Calls show as real calls on phones: on iPhone in the Dynamic Island, lock screen and Control Center; on Android as an ongoing call with Mute and Hang up.
  • Draw a diagram together in a call, end-to-end encrypted: everyone sees it on the stage, others can ask to edit, and it saves as a draw.io file.
  • A Gallery shows every picture and video shared in a channel or DM as a grid.
  • Faster reconnects and less memory for animated pictures, plus a security fix for direct message keys.

v0.7.0 25 Nov 2026

  • Server settings have separate Emoji and Stickers pages: drag in several pictures at once, see them large with their names before uploading, and click any one to rename, move or delete it.
  • Someone sharing camera and screen keeps their camera: a small picture on the screen you can drag to any corner, pop out into its own tile or shrink.
  • Voice channels can be set up to 320 kbps audio quality for listening to music together.
  • The call connection panel is easier to read and shows the channel's audio quality.
  • Shared app sound bars move clearly even at low volume, managers see empty categories again, and "Change my nickname" is no longer confused with renaming the server.

v0.6.0 25 Nov 2026

  • Status works across devices: an idle phone no longer sets you Away while you use your computer, using other apps keeps you Online, and a status you pick on one device applies on all of them.
  • New status: Do not disturb, with a red dot for others and no notifications or message sounds on any of your devices.
  • Press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on Mac) to jump to a person, a channel on any server, or a settings page.
  • Shared app sound shows under the sharer in the voice channel: click to listen, set its volume, and see small bars move while it plays. Animated profile pictures play while someone speaks.
  • Full screen streams keep all call and stream controls, shown on pointer movement, and Escape leaves full screen.
  • Chat: names line up with the avatar and text sits right under them, reactions light up on hover and show the real emoji, and right-clicking message text opens the message menu.
  • Server owners choose file limits and how many messages each channel keeps (or keep them all) in Server settings, unless the host set a cap.
  • Admins can preview the server as one of their own roles, without being able to change anything while previewing.
  • Server settings: the audit log records more changes and shows what changed, saving roles is faster and keeps edits that failed, empty categories are hidden, and a voice channel limit can be removed again.

v0.5.0 25 Nov 2026

  • React to any message with an emoji, end-to-end encrypted. Right-click or long-press pictures, GIFs and stickers to copy, save or open them.
  • Phones: swipe in from the left edge for the channel list, call buttons sit in the call view, and the camera button opens a menu to flip it, switch the light or stop it.
  • Android: calls keep running when you switch apps or lock the screen, Back closes menus and pages first, and files save to Downloads. iOS can share files.
  • Android test builds install as a separate app, so they no longer block installing the release app.
  • Recovery words are hidden from screenshots and the recent apps view on phones.
  • "Request control" is greyed out with the reason when the sharer turned control requests off.
  • Server settings: unsaved changes ask before they are thrown away, deleting an emoji asks first, the audit log loads only on its own page, and the emoji, members and joining pages work on phones.
  • Roles and moderation: invite links stop working when their creator leaves or loses the right to invite, only higher ranks lift a ban, role managers can't strip roles above their reach, and in a DM only the author can delete a message.
  • Proxy, Tor and WireGuard problems show short, translated messages. Dialogs scroll instead of getting cut off on short windows.
  • Security fixes: channel managers only see and manage channels they can read, slow uploads can't block a server, and a flood of device removals can't stop key sync.

v0.4.0 24 Nov 2026

  • Servers come online faster when you connect or reconnect, and RustTalk reconnects within about 10 seconds after a server restart or Wi-Fi hiccup. Others see you come online sooner.
  • A dropped connection is noticed and restored, also during voice calls. Pictures, files and messages that were still transferring are tried again once the network is back.
  • Joining a voice channel is faster, chat keeps working while it connects, and voice calls use less CPU and battery.
  • Long chats and member lists on big servers scroll smoothly, and new messages show up faster.
  • Avatars, server icons and custom emoji are kept in an encrypted cache, so they show right away after a restart. Picture-heavy channels no longer download the same thumbnails again and again.
  • Your messages show up right away while they are sent; one that fails stays in the chat with Try again.
  • Changing your name, picture or bio, or editing channels, emoji and events, no longer makes everyone’s app reload the whole server.
  • Pictures, link previews and big files load and upload in the background, so chat, status and calls keep going meanwhile.
  • Servers keep up better when many people are online, and many devices behind one home router can sign in at once.
  • Loading many things from a server at once is faster: requests share one HTTP/2 connection.

v0.3.0 24 Nov 2026

  • Put a voice channel into focus mode for up to 24 hours: it gets a Focus badge, a countdown banner and a short notice before anyone joins.
  • Channels only some roles can see show a lock, and their permissions are easier to set.
  • Voice channels can have a member limit; the channel list shows how full they are, like 3/5.
  • The stream badge shows a compact viewer count and leaves the video stats visible.
  • Choose Direct, SOCKS5, app-only WireGuard, or Tor during onboarding and in Settings → Network on desktop and mobile, with per-device settings and secure credential storage.
  • Open a profile from a message author’s name or picture.
  • A temporary error syncing your account picture no longer disconnects the server.
  • The bottom-left account picture now lines up with your name and status.
  • Your account picture now follows you to new servers and repairs missing pictures after reconnecting.
  • Rounded-square avatars across the app, a speaking highlight on your bottom-left avatar during voice calls, and subtle hover feedback on sidebar server icons.
  • Incoming live file offers now have a single Download button.
  • Screen shares show simpler controls, preview automatically while focused, and start with system audio selected where supported.
  • Direct messages and mentions play a short chime from RustTalk itself, also when the system keeps notifications quiet; turn it off in Call sounds.
  • In the direct message list, the red badge no longer covers the server names, a server name shows only once, and the unread mark is no longer cut off.
  • While you are in a voice call, RustTalk keeps you Online even when another app has the focus.
  • Reopen the last chat picture instantly, even if the connection drops after it loads.
  • Unavailable servers show an exclamation mark in the server sidebar until they reconnect.
  • Recover voice calls and watched video after a server restart, keeping mute settings and asking again before shared audio plays.
  • Phone calls sound clearer with HD voice (G.722) when your provider supports it, and fall back to normal phone quality otherwise.
  • "Always allow" shares your microphone with every phone call without asking; turn it off again in Settings > Privacy.
  • Others in the channel see a phone number you typed only as its last two digits; contacts show their name.

v0.2.1 23 Nov 2026

  • Incoming custom emoji now appear as soon as their pictures finish loading, without waiting for another chat message.
  • Backgrounding Android during a camera switch no longer waits for the camera or reopens it after pause.
  • Retry a failed forum post with its title, body and poll. Replies stay visible when a poll and its post arrive separately.
  • Forum channels: searchable posts with folded replies, polls, up and down votes and sorting.
  • Close image previews by clicking or tapping the dimmed space around them.
  • Oversized stored uploads show their size and the app limit beside chat or shared files, including voice channels.
  • Screen shares use the sharer’s participant card, show distinct viewer counts, and preview your screen while the window is focused.
  • Mobile calls show your camera preview with front/back switching and a supported rear-camera light.
  • Shared app and screen audio stays silent until you choose Listen; Stop listening ends it immediately.
  • Kicking a member now asks for confirmation by name and keeps the original member and server selected.
  • Ask someone to join your voice channel: it rings, and they can join, decline, come later or mute you for 15 minutes
  • Servers can turn on Events: a calendar above the channels to suggest, approve and add events, in real life or in a channel
  • Say whether you are going to an event, see who is still missing, and remind them
  • Event reminders reach unanswered authors and respect “Only when RustTalk isn’t focused” without repeating later
  • Channel names show the pointing-hand cursor when hovered, including for channel managers.
  • Desktop: call a phone number into your voice channel with your own SIP account, contacts and a keypad. Phone calls use unencrypted SIP/RTP; the provider and network path can hear them.
  • Listen to phone audio separately from sharing your microphone with the phone. The caller shares their own microphone; other members must consent; stopping sharing pauses the channel microphone until exclusion is confirmed or the call ends.
  • Phone calls end on reconnect or lost permission; hold and one-way audio are not supported.
